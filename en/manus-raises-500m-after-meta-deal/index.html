Manus raises over $500M in first round since Meta deal collapsed

Boyu Capital and IDG Capital led the round for the AI agent startup, which China ordered to unwind its sale to Meta in April.

2026-10-08 · EmaritesAI

Butterfly Effect, the parent company of AI agent maker Manus, said on Thursday in a WeChat post that it has raised more than $500 million. Boyu Capital and IDG Capital led the round, and existing shareholders including Tencent, HSG (formerly Sequoia China) and ZhenFund took part. The company says it will keep hiring in China and abroad.

It is Manus's first funding since its sale to Meta fell apart. Meta announced the deal in December 2025, at a reported value of about $2 billion. In April, China's National Development and Reform Commission blocked it, and in August Manus said it was operating independently again. TechCrunch reports that Manus said it had to delete some user data as part of the split.

Manus did not disclose a valuation. TechCrunch notes that last month Manus was reported to be in talks to raise $500 million at a $4 billion valuation, and that the company is reported to be considering a listing in Hong Kong.

Manus became widely known in 2025 after a demo of its general AI agent. It now sells agent and app-building tools similar to those from Cursor, Lovable and Replit. It recently launched Manus 2.0 and Cue, an app that gives personal AI agents their own email addresses, phone numbers, digital wallets and computers, with spending limits set by the user.

3 things to know
  1. Manus's parent raised over $500M, led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital
  2. China blocked Meta's planned $2B purchase of Manus in April
  3. No valuation given; a $4B figure was reported in talks last month
