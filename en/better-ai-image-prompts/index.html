Get better AI images in 5 steps: subject, light, lens and size

Google's prompt guide for its Imagen models shows how a few precise words change what an image generator draws.

2026-10-12 · EmaritesAI

New image models such as Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo can turn out a picture in a few steps, but the result still depends on what you ask for. Google's prompt guide for its Imagen models, published in its Vertex AI documentation, offers a structure that works across most tools.

Start with three parts: the subject, the context around it and the style. "A sketch of a modern apartment building surrounded by skyscrapers" covers all three. Then add detail one round at a time instead of writing a long prompt at once, and compare each result.

Lighting words such as warm, cold, natural or dramatic change the mood. Camera terms help too: close-up or aerial for position, and lens types such as macro for small objects or wide angle for big outdoor scenes. For realistic photos, the guide suggests matching the focal length to the subject, for example 60 to 105mm macro for food.

Pick the aspect ratio for where the image will go: 1:1 for a feed post, 9:16 for stories and reels, 16:9 for wide scenes. If you want text in the image, keep it to 25 characters or fewer and expect to regenerate a few times.

Step by step
  1. Subject, context, style: Say what, where and in which style, such as a sketch, photo or oil painting.
  2. Build in rounds: Start short, then add one detail at a time and compare the results.
  3. Set the light: Use words like warm, cold, natural or dramatic lighting to set the mood.
  4. Pick the lens: Ask for close-up, aerial, macro or wide angle to control the framing.
  5. Size and text: Match the aspect ratio to the platform and keep on-image text under 25 characters.
3 things to know
  1. Name the subject, the setting and the style first
  2. Add light and lens words one round at a time
  3. Keep text in images to 25 characters or fewer
Sources: Google Cloud: Imagen prompt and image attribute guide, Hugging Face: Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo
⚠️ Guide recommends subject, context and style, built up iteratively
⚠️ Lens advice incl. 60-105mm macro for food
⚠️ Keep in-image text to 25 characters or fewer
⚠️ Aspect ratio uses: 1:1 social, 9:16 short-form video, 16:9 landscapes
