Turn your AI chatbot into a personal tutor in 4 steps

Set the chat up to coach you, so you keep the skill when the tool is closed.

2026-10-11 · EmaritesAI

AI assistants are fast at giving answers, and that speed can work against learning. A Berkeley-led study presented this week found that people who used ChatGPT for about 10 minutes became less accurate and skipped more problems once the tool was removed. The authors suggest AI should behave more like a tutor.

You can set that up yourself. Start each session by telling the assistant what you are learning, your level, and that you want hints rather than solutions. Ask it to wait for your attempt before it says more.

Some tools have this built in. OpenAI added a study mode to ChatGPT in July 2025 that uses questions, hints and knowledge checks instead of giving answers outright. You can switch it on from the tools menu by choosing "Study and learn".

The second habit is to explain the idea back in your own words and ask the assistant to point out what you missed. Then ask for fresh practice questions and check your answers only after you have tried them all.

Finally, close the chat now and then. Solving one problem a day without help shows you what you can actually do alone, which is the skill you came to build.

Step by step
  1. Ask for hints first: Tell the AI your level and ask for one hint at a time, waiting for your attempt.
  2. Turn on a study mode: ChatGPT's "Study and learn" tool guides you with questions instead of answers.
  3. Explain it back: Put the idea in your own words and ask the AI to point out gaps or errors.
  4. Practise, then work alone: Request new questions, check answers after trying all, and solve one daily unaided.
3 things to know
  1. Ask the AI for hints and to wait for your attempt
  2. Explaining ideas back exposes what you have not understood
  3. Regular practice without AI keeps the skill yours
Sources: OpenAI: Introducing study mode, Berkeley News
✅ Berkeley-led study: ~10 min of ChatGPT reduced accuracy/persistence after removal
⚠️ ChatGPT study mode launched July 29, 2025, uses questions, hints, knowledge checks; 'Study and learn' in tools
