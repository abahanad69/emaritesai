Nvidia's open Nemotron models reach gold level at IOI and IMO 2026

An unofficial run scored 535.4 of 600 at the programming olympiad, and official graders gave the math system 30 of 42.

2026-10-08 · EmaritesAI

Nvidia says fine-tuned versions of its open Nemotron 3 Ultra model reached gold-medal level at both the 2026 International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI) and the 2026 International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO).

At IOI, a model called Nemotron-3-Ultra-CC scored 535.4 out of 600 in a live run held under the same time, internet and submission limits as the human contestants. The gold threshold was 361.12 and the top human score was 498.27. Nvidia notes the run was unofficial and unsupervised, so it does not appear in the IOI rankings.

At IMO, a generate, verify and refine system built on Nemotron 3 Ultra checkpoints scored 30 out of 42, with full marks on four of the six problems. The gold threshold was 29. Nvidia says the proofs were graded by official IMO graders and that the system worked in natural language only, without a formal prover, external tools or internet access. For IOI the team trained on 22,000 curated problems with synthetic reasoning traces, and the IMO model learned from 414,890 examples across 15,818 proof problems.

Nvidia has published the model, the training checkpoints, both training datasets and a set of 200 olympiad-level problems on Hugging Face, with the inference pipelines in its NeMo-Skills repository.

3 things to know
  1. Nemotron-3-Ultra-CC scored 535.4/600 at IOI 2026 (gold: 361.12)
  2. Its IMO system scored 30/42, above the gold threshold of 29
  3. Nvidia released the checkpoints and datasets on Hugging Face
Sources: Hugging Face (NVIDIA): Nemotron for IOI and IMO
