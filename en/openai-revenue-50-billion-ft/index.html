OpenAI's revenue run rate is near $50B, $20B below earlier reports

The Financial Times says OpenAI told investors its annualised revenue is approaching $50 billion, not the $70 billion reported last month.

2026-10-09 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI has told investors that its annualised revenue is approaching $50 billion, according to the Financial Times, as reported by TechCrunch. That is about $20 billion below the roughly $70 billion figure Axios reported at the end of September.

The FT says the $70 billion number was devised by investors to compare OpenAI with Anthropic. The two companies count annualised revenue differently: Anthropic includes sales made through its cloud partners, while OpenAI does not. That difference alone can move the headline figure a long way.

Annualised revenue is a projection based on recent sales, not money already earned over a year. It is the number AI labs now use most often to show growth to investors.

The gap matters because of the sums involved. OpenAI raised $122 billion in a funding round in March 2026, and TechCrunch reports that its planned stock market listing, once expected this year, has moved to early 2027. Leaked figures for 2025 showed about $13 billion in revenue, with spending well above that.

TechCrunch said it asked OpenAI for comment. No response was included in its report.

  3. OpenAI and Anthropic count annualised revenue differently
