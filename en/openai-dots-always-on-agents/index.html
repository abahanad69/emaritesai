OpenAI launches Dots, always-on AI agents that keep working for you

Unveiled at DevDay, Dots run on GPT-6 Astra with their own cloud computer and 4,000+ apps.

2026-09-30 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI unveiled Dots at its DevDay conference in San Francisco on Tuesday: personal agents designed to take on a goal and keep working on it in the background. Chief executive Sam Altman described them as always-on agents that "can handle really anything you can think of".

Dots are powered by GPT-6 Astra. According to The Verge, each one uses its own cloud computer with a web browser and more than 4,000 supported apps, and users can watch its progress on that machine. You name your Dot, chat with it in a text-message-style interface or call it by voice in ChatGPT, and reach it from Slack and Microsoft Teams, with text messages coming soon.

For now each user gets one Dot; OpenAI plans teams of specialist Dots later, provisioned with their own identities, credentials and tools. It is working with Microsoft to connect them to Agent 365 security controls. A new shared workspace, ChatGPT Space, lets colleagues work with a Dot together.

Dots are available from Tuesday in ChatGPT for Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets, and can be launched from ChatGPT or Codex.

The launch answers Meta's Muse and arrives a day after OpenAI withheld GPT-6.1 Astra over safety concerns.

3 things to know
  1. Always-on agents powered by GPT-6 Astra
  2. Own cloud computer, browser and 4,000+ apps
  3. Live now for ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium users
Sources: OpenAI, TechCrunch, The Verge, BBC
