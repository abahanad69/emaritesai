ChatGPT adds virtual try-on so you can see clothes on yourself

OpenAI's new shopping tools use its Images 2.5 model and a Favorites library.

2026-10-02 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI is pushing further into online shopping. The company has launched two features for ChatGPT globally: virtual try-on, which shows clothing and accessories on a photo of you, and Favorites, a place to save products for later.

Try-on works from a selfie or a full-body photo. A new Try On button appears in ChatGPT's shopping results, and you can also upload an image of an item, such as a screenshot from a store's website, and ask ChatGPT to put it on you. Saved products and try-on images live together in the app's Library.

OpenAI says the features are powered by its recently launched ChatGPT Images 2.5 model, which it claims produces more natural lighting and textures, follows edits more reliably and generates images faster.

The move matters because shopping is one of the clearest ways for AI assistants to make money, and a crowded one. Google introduced virtual try-on last year, and Pinterest remains a major source of fashion inspiration. TechCrunch notes that OpenAI previously had to rethink an instant checkout feature that did not perform well.

Users can also describe a style and ask ChatGPT to find pieces for the full look, or upload an outfit photo to find similar items for sale. As always with AI-generated images, the result is an approximation; fit and colour may differ from the real product.

Before uploading a full-body photo, it is worth checking ChatGPT's data and privacy settings.

3 things to know
  1. ChatGPT can show clothes and accessories on your own photo
  2. A Favorites library saves products alongside try-on images
  3. Powered by OpenAI's new ChatGPT Images 2.5 model
