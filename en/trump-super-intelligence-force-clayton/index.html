Trump creates 'Super Intelligence Force' led by Jay Clayton

The new White House task force will coordinate federal AI policy and reportedly has 120 days to report on risks and opportunities.

2026-10-05 · EmaritesAI

US President Donald Trump has set up a federal task force on artificial intelligence, which he named the Super Intelligence Force, in a Truth Social post on Sunday. Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton will chair it.

The other named members are Federal Trade Commission Chair Andrew Ferguson, the Pentagon's research and engineering chief Emil Michael, and Office of Personnel Management Director Scott Kupor. According to the BBC, the group reports directly to the president and to White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles.

Trump wrote that the force will coordinate the government's dealings with consumers, public interest groups, religious organisations, critical infrastructure providers and AI companies, and work to keep the US ahead in the technology.

The Wall Street Journal, as cited by TechCrunch, reports that the task force has 120 days to produce a report on the risks and opportunities of AI. Its charter reportedly calls for plans to respond to AI-enabled threats while avoiding overregulation. Clayton told the paper that one of the biggest risks is not being first.

The move comes days after executives from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others signed a non-binding safety pact at the White House on 29 September, which Trump called morally binding. He also signed an order telling officials to call AI "super intelligence". Elon Musk said on Sunday that SpaceX will rename its AI platform SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI.

The 120-day report would land in early 2027.

3 things to know
  1. DNI Jay Clayton chairs the new federal AI task force
  2. FTC chair Andrew Ferguson and Pentagon R&D chief Emil Michael are members
  3. A report on AI risks and opportunities is reportedly due in 120 days
