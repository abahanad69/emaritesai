NVIDIA raises Shield TV Pro price by $100, citing memory costs

The seven-year-old streaming box now costs $299, and the cheaper standard model has been dropped.

2026-10-04 · EmaritesAI

NVIDIA has raised the price of its Shield TV Pro streaming box by $100, to $299.99, starting October 2. The cheaper standard Shield TV has been discontinued.

The current Shield models launched in 2019 at $199.99 for the Pro and $149.99 for the standard version. They run Android TV on an older Tegra X1+ chip and are known for AI upscaling and wide video format support.

An NVIDIA spokesperson told Ars Technica that the cost of components, including memory, has risen substantially across the industry. NVIDIA still manufactures the Shield, so new units are exposed to current parts prices. Stock has been thin in recent months, and the box remains out of stock on NVIDIA's own store, though retailers list it at the new price.

The increase is a small example of a wider trend. AI data centers are buying huge volumes of memory chips, and that demand is lifting prices for phones, PCs and consoles. Sony's PS5 Pro, for example, has gone from $699.99 at launch in 2024 to $899.99 today. Memory maker Micron said this week that the shortage could last through 2028.

There is an irony in the story: NVIDIA's AI accelerators are a major driver of that data center demand, and now its own consumer hardware is paying the price.

3 things to know
  1. Shield TV Pro rises from $199.99 to $299.99, effective October 2.
  2. NVIDIA cites higher component costs, including memory.
  3. The cheaper standard Shield TV has been discontinued.
