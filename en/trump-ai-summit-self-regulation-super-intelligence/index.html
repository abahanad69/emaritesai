Trump and AI chiefs sign self-regulation pact at the White House

Tech leaders signed a 'morally binding' safety document; an executive order renames AI in federal usage.

2026-09-30 · EmaritesAI

President Donald Trump hosted a White House lunch on Tuesday with leaders from OpenAI, Anthropic, Nvidia, Meta, Google and SpaceX, alongside House Speaker Mike Johnson, the BBC and The Verge report.

Afterwards, Trump said the executives had signed a "morally binding" document. Under the text, which he posted online, companies are responsible for the safety of their own technology, will implement safeguards to keep models operating as intended, and will work with independent auditors to check that their systems do not hack or access technical systems in unintended ways. It was signed by Trump, Sundar Pichai, Dario Amodei, Mark Zuckerberg, OpenAI president Greg Brockman, Elon Musk and Jensen Huang.

Trump said he would set up a board to oversee AI safety but did not say who would sit on it. He repeated that he favours industry self-regulation.

The same day, he signed an executive order directing federal agencies to use the term "Super Intelligence" instead of "artificial intelligence" on official websites, policy documents and press releases. Past regulations and documents do not have to change, according to The Verge.

The meeting follows weeks of calls from OpenAI and Anthropic for tighter oversight after a series of rogue-agent incidents.

  3. Federal agencies must now say 'Super Intelligence'
