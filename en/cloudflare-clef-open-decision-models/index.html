Cloudflare releases Clef, open-weight AI models built to decide

Clef and Clef-flash return choices with confidence scores instead of free text.

2026-10-02 · EmaritesAI

Cloudflare has launched Clef and Clef-flash, two open-weight decision models, and made them available on its Workers AI platform and on Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 licence. The company also announced a reinforcement learning service for fine-tuning Clef, offered for now through its engineers rather than self-serve.

A decision model does not write essays. Given an input, such as a support ticket, it chooses from a fixed list of answers and attaches a probability to each one. That makes it suited to the many small yes-or-no and routing steps inside AI agent workflows, where a full chatbot is slower, costlier and less predictable.

Cloudflare says Clef is built on Qwen open models, supports a 64K-token context and can also classify images. In its own tests, it reports a median latency of 209 milliseconds for Clef and 38.8 milliseconds for Clef-flash, and says Clef currently leads the Jev Decision Index. Those results are vendor-reported. Internally, Cloudflare says it uses Clef to categorise websites for threat intelligence.

The release reflects a crowded new niche. Decision models took off after startup TypeSafe introduced Jev, and AWS released its own open model, Strands Decider 2B, the same week. Clef is also designed to be compatible with Jev's API, making it easier to swap in.

Cloudflare has not published separate pricing for Clef in its announcement.
