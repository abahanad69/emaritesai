OpenAI brings image ads to ChatGPT, starting with US image generation

Sponsored product pictures will appear next to AI-generated images in a US test later this month.

2026-10-06 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI announced on Monday a new visual ad format for ChatGPT. The ads show product images with a "Learn more" button and will first appear while users generate images in the chatbot. Testing begins later this month in the United States with an initial group of advertisers.

OpenAI says the ads are clearly labelled, stay separate from the image being created and do not affect ChatGPT's answers. According to The Verge, they will not appear for Plus, Pro or Enterprise subscribers. Until now, ChatGPT's ads showed only a brand name, logo and link in a sponsored section under a reply. The company also says it keeps ads out of emotionally sensitive conversations.

OpenAI began showing ads in ChatGPT earlier this year to fund its free and low-cost tiers, and TechCrunch reports they reached India in August. The company says ChatGPT has 1.2 billion weekly users.

Alongside the new format, OpenAI added more measurement partners for advertisers, including AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch and Northbeam, and started brand-safety pilots with DoubleVerify and Integral Ad Science. Advertisers can also use geography-based tests with partners such as Haus to check whether the ads drive extra sales.

OpenAI says it plans to test more ad formats in the future.

3 things to know
  1. Image ads will appear while users generate pictures in ChatGPT
  2. The US test starts later this month with a small advertiser group
  3. Plus, Pro and Enterprise subscribers won't see ads, per The Verge
