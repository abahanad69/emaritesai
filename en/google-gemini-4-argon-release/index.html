Google releases Gemini 4 Argon at $2 per 1M input tokens

Google's first major frontier model since Gemini 3 is rolling out to developers, enterprises and paying Gemini subscribers at an introductory price.

2026-10-14 · EmaritesAI

Google made Gemini 4 Argon generally available on October 13. Developers and companies can call it through the Gemini API in AI Studio and the Gemini platform, Google AI Ultra subscribers get it in the Antigravity coding tool, and both Pro and Ultra subscribers can use it in the Gemini app and in AI Mode in Search.

The launch price is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input 95% cheaper. The Verge reports that these rates double at the end of November, when the introductory period ends. It is Google's first major frontier release since Gemini 3 in late 2025, and it arrived one day after Meta launched Muse Wonder.

Google says Argon ranks first on the Vals Index, which scores models on finance, coding, legal and tax work, and that it beats rival frontier models on its own coding, science and cybersecurity tests. Inside Google, the company says Argon raised agentic code submissions by more than 35% and cut code rollbacks by nearly a third. For robotics, Google reports an 87% score on the ERQA spatial reasoning benchmark.

Google's safety report says an unguarded Argon solved all 12 simulated cyberattack scenarios in its tests, up from five for the previous generation. Because of that jump, Google added extra protections for cyber and biology requests and is widening early access for vetted security teams through its Fairwind Program. All benchmark figures come from Google.
