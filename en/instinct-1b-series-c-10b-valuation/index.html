AI agent Instinct raises $1B at a $10B valuation

The Series C comes a month after a $2.5B valuation, from investors including Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue.

2026-09-29 · EmaritesAI

Instinct, the startup behind a viral consumer AI agent, has raised $1 billion in a Series C round that values it at $10 billion, TechCrunch reports. Investors include Sequoia Capital, Benchmark and Coatue.

The round comes only a month after Instinct announced a raise at a $2.5 billion valuation, a fast pace for a company that launched its invite-only service in August 2026.

Instinct belongs to a new class of agents that do more than chat: they book travel and restaurants, make purchases, pay bills, cancel subscriptions and order groceries. When given a task, Instinct uses its own phone number and computer. Recent features include a concierge that makes phone calls for appointments that cannot be booked online, and a "trusted person network" that lets one user's agent coordinate plans with friends' agents. On the same day, Shopify announced a direct partnership with Instinct for agent shopping.

Those capabilities require users to share a lot of personal information, and Instinct's first privacy policy drew criticism for overreach; it has since been updated.

The startup now faces direct competition from Meta's Muse, which offers similar features plus deep integration with Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp.

3 things to know
  1. $1B Series C at a $10B valuation
  2. Valuation quadrupled in about a month
  3. Agent uses its own phone number and computer for tasks
