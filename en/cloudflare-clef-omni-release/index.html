Cloudflare's open Clef-omni scores audio and video for $0.15 per 1M

The new model picks between fixed options from text, images, audio or video, and Cloudflare cut Clef-flash's price to $0.038.

2026-10-12 · EmaritesAI

Cloudflare added a third model to its Clef family on October 9. Clef-omni accepts audio in WAV or MP3, video in MP4 or WebM, images and text, and instead of writing an answer it returns schema-constrained scores for a fixed set of options. That makes it a fit for jobs such as moderation, routing and classification, where a system needs a quick decision rather than prose.

According to Cloudflare, Clef-omni is built on Alibaba's Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct, a mixture-of-experts model, with the speech output parts removed. The weights are on Hugging Face and the model runs on Workers AI at a launch price of $0.15 per million input tokens. Cloudflare reports median latency of about 130 milliseconds for text and 150 for images, and says a 21-second video with sound took about 1.5 seconds.

The company published its own scores: 94.8 macro-F1 on the BANKING77 intent test and 97.7 on CLINC150 with out-of-scope queries. These are vendor figures and have not been independently checked.

The update also changes the two models released a week earlier. Clef-flash now costs $0.038 per million input tokens, down from $0.09, but its hosted context window drops from 64k to 24k tokens; Cloudflare says only 0.24% of requests went over that limit. Clef keeps its $0.24 price and now serves responses 1.7 to 2 times faster at the median.
