TII launches Falcon-Emirati, a 7B model that speaks Emirati Arabic

Abu Dhabi's Technology Innovation Institute tuned its Falcon-H1-Arabic model for the dialect, its idioms and its culture.

2026-10-07 · EmaritesAI

The Technology Innovation Institute (TII) in Abu Dhabi has released Falcon-Emirati-7B, a language model built to understand and write Emirati Arabic as people in the UAE speak it. Most Arabic models are trained mainly on Modern Standard Arabic, which is rarely used in daily conversation.

The model is a 7-billion-parameter version of Falcon-H1-Arabic, TII's Arabic family that mixes Mamba state-space layers with transformer attention. TII says it chose the 7B size as a balance between quality and the cost of training and running the model. It can be tried in TII's Falcon chat interface.

Emirati is mostly spoken, so there is little written text to learn from. TII says it collected dialect text from Emirati websites and forums, added Standard Arabic material about Emirati customs and heritage, and generated synthetic dialect data under strict glossaries and grammar rules. Native speakers reviewed results throughout.

According to TII, the model scored 84.83% on the 1,173-question Alyah benchmark, ahead of every Arabic and multilingual model it compared. On the UAE part of the ArabCulture-Dialogue test it scored 85.57%, against 83.39% for ALLaM-7B and 73.79% for Jais-2-8B. TII also found that rival models often answered in Standard Arabic when prompted in Emirati.

These are TII's own results and have not been independently replicated.

3 things to know
  1. TII's Falcon-Emirati-7B is tuned for the Emirati Arabic dialect
  2. Built on Falcon-H1-Arabic; try it in TII's Falcon chat
  3. TII reports 84.83% on the Alyah benchmark, top of models it tested
