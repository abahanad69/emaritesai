Crusoe raises $3.9B for data centers and truckable AI factories

The Series F values the eight-year-old data center developer at $30.9 billion.

2026-09-19 · EmaritesAI

Crusoe said it raised $3.9 billion in a Series F round that pushes its valuation to $30.9 billion, one of the largest rounds yet for the physical layer of the AI boom.

The round was co-led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital and Valor Equity Partners, with Founders Fund, GIC, Nvidia, the Qatar Investment Authority, Radical Ventures and TPG also taking part. Crusoe named three new board members: Cloudflare finance chief Thomas Seifert, Primary Digital Infrastructure partner Bill Stein, and Redwood Materials founder JB Straubel, an investor in the company since 2021.

Part of the capital goes to projects already under way, including the large Abilene, Texas site used by OpenAI. The rest funds Spark, Crusoe's line of small modular data centers built in its own factories, shipped by truck and plugged into large power sources almost anywhere.

That design answers two constraints at once. Building at a factory rather than on site removes the need for a large construction workforce, and smaller sites are easier to place in communities that have begun pushing back against sprawling data center complexes.

Crusoe leases capacity to customers who bring their own hardware. Chief executive Chase Lochmiller said reaching an era of abundance means "controlling the infrastructure from electrons to tokens."
