Nscale raises $3.36B in convertible notes ahead of NYSE IPO

Third Point leads the round; Nvidia adds $1 billion due in mid-November.

2026-09-28 · EmaritesAI

Nscale, a British provider of AI cloud capacity, has secured $3.36 billion in financing ahead of its initial public offering later this year, the company announced, TechCrunch reports.

The round is structured as convertible notes and led by hedge fund Third Point. It includes $2.36 billion available to Nscale immediately and a further $1 billion from existing investor Nvidia, due in mid-November. The notes convert into shares once the IPO is complete.

Nscale filed its IPO paperwork last week. The Financial Times reported that it is expected to be valued at $35 billion on the New York Stock Exchange, and Bloomberg reported that it is seeking to raise $3 billion in the offering.

The company was spun out of Australian cryptocurrency miner Arkon Energy two years ago and, according to its filing, has since signed more than $103 billion in contracts. It is building several large data-centre campuses, including sites in Norway and West Virginia.

The raise underlines how much capital the AI build-out now demands, and how central Nvidia has become as both supplier and investor to the neoclouds that rent its chips out. Crusoe, another data-centre developer, raised $3.9 billion earlier this month.

3 things to know
  1. $3.36B convertible financing led by Third Point
  2. $1B of it comes from Nvidia in mid-November
  3. Reported $35B valuation target for its NYSE listing
