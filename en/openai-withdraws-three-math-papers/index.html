OpenAI withdraws 3 AI-written math papers after a sign error

One sign error broke a key argument in a paper on Weil classes and in two papers that built on it.

2026-10-08 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI has withdrawn three papers from openai/math, its public GitHub collection of mathematics produced by an internal, unreleased OpenAI model. A changelog dated 7 October says a sign error invalidates a cancellation argument in "Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds", and that two other papers relied on the same construction.

The two dependent papers are "Algebraicity of Kuga-Satake Correspondences for K3 Surfaces" and "The rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces". The Hodge conjecture is one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems, and the third title claimed a special case of it. Each withdrawn paper now carries a notice explaining the gap, with a link to the archived manuscript.

The same update revised 14 other manuscripts with repaired proofs, corrected statements and clearer hypotheses, including six papers on Kähler minimal model programs. Another 13 papers were changed only to cite the corrected companion work.

OpenAI also added six formal proofs, which are checked by software in the Lean language. It says 300 of the collection's 719 top-line results, about 42%, are now formalized. The repository's own README warns that results without a formal proof could still contain errors and says OpenAI will try to fix them quickly.

For readers, the takeaway is simple: until a result is formally checked or reviewed by experts, treat an AI-generated proof as a draft.
