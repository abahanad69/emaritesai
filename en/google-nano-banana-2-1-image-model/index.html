Google's Nano Banana 2.1 makes 4K images from up to 14 references

The new Flash-tier image model improves text in pictures and infographic accuracy, and is live in the Gemini app and API.

2026-10-08 · EmaritesAI

Google has released Nano Banana 2.1, the successor to Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image) in its fast Flash tier. Google DeepMind published the model card on 6 October, and the model is listed as stable in the Gemini API under the ID gemini-nano-banana-2.1.

It makes and edits images at 1K, 2K and 4K. Google's developer documentation lists better realism at all three sizes, a fix for tiling artifacts on very wide or tall formats such as 8:1, and better text and infographic layouts. It can combine up to 14 reference images, keeping up to four characters or ten objects consistent, and it can ground images in Google Web and Image Search. Users can set thinking to minimal, medium or high.

On Google's own human preference tests, it scores an Elo of 1,050 for text-to-image, against 990 for Gemini 3.1 Flash Image and 935 for Gemini 3 Pro Image. Its infographic factuality score is 0.521, up from 0.179 for the previous Flash model. The model card also lists weak spots: small or dense text, some character drift, and confusion between left and right.

Nano Banana 2.1 is available in the Gemini app, Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, AI Mode in Search, Google Ads, Flow and Stitch. Google's model page does not list a price.
