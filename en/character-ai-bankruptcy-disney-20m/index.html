Character.AI files for bankruptcy, agrees $20M asset sale to Disney

Disney's offer sets the floor for a court-run auction, and the chatbot app keeps running while a buyer is found.

2026-10-13 · EmaritesAI

Character.AI, one of the first consumer hits of the generative AI wave, has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in the United States. Its owner, Character Technologies, said on October 12 that it agreed to sell some of its assets, including a license to certain intellectual property, to Walt Disney for $20 million.

Disney is the stalking-horse bidder. Its offer sets a minimum price, and other buyers can submit higher bids in an auction the company has asked the court to approve. Character.AI expects the sale to be completed in late November or early December. A court filing puts the company's estimated assets and liabilities each in the range of $50 million to $100 million.

The chatbot and roleplay platform will keep running with minimal disruption during the process, the company said. If no buyer agrees to operate it, the business will be wound down. Chief executive Colin Adams said the board chose a court-supervised sale after reviewing other options.

The filing follows a long decline from the company's peak. In 2024 its founders and staff joined Google in a deal reported at $2.7 billion, after Google took a non-exclusive license to Character.AI's language model technology. The company then stopped building its own large models and focused on its chatbot app. It has also faced lawsuits over chatbot safety, including a May 2026 case from Pennsylvania alleging that a bot presented itself as a licensed psychiatrist.
