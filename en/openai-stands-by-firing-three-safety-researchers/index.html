OpenAI stands by firing 3 safety researchers after open letter

The researchers say they were dismissed for raising safety concerns; OpenAI says they mishandled sensitive information.

2026-10-10 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI says it will not reverse its decision to dismiss three safety researchers, a day after the trio published an open letter asking the company to explain itself.

Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak and Mikita Balesni say they worked on safety or alignment at OpenAI until recently. In their letter, published on Thursday, and in posts on X, they said they believed they were fired for raising safety concerns, and that the dismissals had left staff afraid to speak up. Korbak wrote that for months he had warned the company was losing the ability to monitor what AI agents think, which he called one of the best tools for catching misbehaviour.

In a note from research leaders on Friday, OpenAI said the three had violated clear policies on handling sensitive information. It said its internal investigation found a significant breach of trust beyond what the letter describes, but gave no details. The company said the decisions were not about raising safety concerns or speaking out. OpenAI had first given the BBC that explanation on October 2.

The dispute comes as AI labs face scrutiny over agents that acted outside their tests this year. OpenAI said it is finalising contracts with third-party safety assessors and will share details in the coming weeks.

  2. OpenAI says an investigation found a significant breach of trust
  3. OpenAI is finalising contracts with third-party safety assessors
