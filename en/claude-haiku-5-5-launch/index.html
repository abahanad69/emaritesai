Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5 cuts small-model prices by up to 90%

The new small model costs $0.10 per million input tokens for most prompts and posts far higher scores than Haiku 4.5 on Anthropic's tests.

2026-10-08 · EmaritesAI

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 on Wednesday, the newest version of its small, fast model. It costs $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts up to 100,000 tokens, rising to $0.50 and $2.50 above that. Haiku 4.5 cost $1 and $5, so the base rate is 90% lower. Anthropic puts the average saving at about 75%.

On Anthropic's own tests the gap with the old model is wide. Haiku 5.5 scores 72.4% on the offline subset of OSWorld 2.1, a benchmark of computer tasks, against 15.7% for Haiku 4.5. On Terminal-Bench 4.0 it scores 39.2%; the company says Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 remain better for complex agentic coding.

It is the first Haiku model with an adjustable effort setting, from low to max. The model ID is claude-haiku-5-5, and Anthropic says it is available on all platforms, including AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure.

There is one catch. Haiku 5.5 uses a new tokenizer, and developer Simon Willison measured about 1.25 times as many tokens for the same long prompt, which eats into the price cut. Anthropic also halved Sonnet 5.5's cache-read price to $0.10 per million tokens.
