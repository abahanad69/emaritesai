5 ways to get fewer made-up answers from AI chatbots

Simple prompt habits that make an assistant admit gaps and show where its answer came from.

2026-10-04 · EmaritesAI

AI chatbots are built to produce a fluent answer, and sometimes that answer includes facts, figures or sources that do not exist. This is called hallucination. You cannot switch it off, but the way you ask makes a real difference.

The first step is to give the assistant permission not to know. Models tend to fill gaps, so saying 'if you are not sure, say so' lowers the pressure to invent.

When you work with a long document, ask for exact quotes before any summary or analysis. Anthropic's guidance for its Claude models recommends this for long files, because it ties the answer to real text.

Ask for a source next to each claim, then remove any claim that has none. If the topic is your own material, tell the assistant to use only the documents you provided and not its general knowledge.

Finally, for anything important, ask the same question twice in fresh chats. If the answers disagree, treat the point as unverified.

None of these steps makes an AI fully reliable. For health, legal, financial or work-critical facts, check the original source yourself before you act on it.

Step by step
  1. Allow 'I don't know': Add 'If you are not sure, say so' to your prompt.
  2. Quotes first: For long files, ask for exact quotes before the summary.
  3. Source every claim: Ask for a source per claim and drop any without one.
  4. Stick to your files: Tell it to use only the documents you provided.
  5. Ask twice: Run the question in two fresh chats and compare the answers.
3 things to know
  1. Telling the AI it may say 'I don't know' reduces invented answers.
  2. Quotes and sources tie each claim to real text you can check.
  3. Asking twice and comparing exposes answers the model is unsure of.
Sources: Anthropic docs: Reduce hallucinations
⚠️ Recommended techniques: allow uncertainty, extract direct quotes for long documents, require citations, restrict to provided documents, best-of-N comparison
⚠️ These techniques reduce but do not eliminate hallucinations
