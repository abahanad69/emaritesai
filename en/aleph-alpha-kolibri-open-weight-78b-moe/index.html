Aleph Alpha releases Kolibri, a free 78B English-German model

The German lab's open-weight model uses only 3.46B active parameters per token and reads up to 1M tokens.

2026-10-04 · EmaritesAI

German AI company Aleph Alpha released Kolibri on October 3, the Day of German Reunification. The model's weights are free to download from Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 license, which allows commercial use.

Kolibri-1 is a mixture-of-experts model trained for English and German. According to its model card it has 78B parameters in total but activates only 3.46B for each token, choosing 6 of 384 experts in every layer. It accepts up to about 1M tokens of context, with 262K recommended for efficiency, and offers reasoning modes plus tool calling.

Aleph Alpha is pitching the model at public administration, industry and aerospace, where data often cannot leave the building. The company says Kolibri can run on a single NVIDIA H200 or B200, or on two H100 cards, so organisations can keep it on their own servers instead of sending data to an outside service.

On the company's own tests, Kolibri scores 75.5 overall in English and 70.8 in German, averages 96.5% on English math benchmarks and reaches 63.2% on the RULER long-context test at 1M tokens. Aleph Alpha says it matches models with up to four times as many active parameters on math, code and long-context work. These figures have not yet been checked independently.

The release follows Kolibri Origin, a smaller 30B test model with a 65K context that Aleph Alpha used to validate its training pipeline.
