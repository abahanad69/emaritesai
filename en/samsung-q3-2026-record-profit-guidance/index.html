Samsung expects record 107.4 trillion won quarterly profit

Preliminary third-quarter figures put operating profit almost nine times higher than a year earlier, as AI chip demand lifts memory sales.

2026-10-08 · EmaritesAI

Samsung Electronics expects to report operating profit of about 107.4 trillion won for July to September 2026, according to earnings guidance it published on Thursday. Sales for the quarter are estimated at about 195 trillion won.

The figures are preliminary. Samsung gives a range of 107.3 to 107.5 trillion won for profit and 194 to 196 trillion won for sales, and says the headline numbers are midpoints because Korean disclosure rules do not allow ranges.

The jump is large. In the same quarter of 2025, Samsung's operating profit was 12.17 trillion won, so the new estimate is almost nine times higher. In the second quarter of 2026 it was 89.49 trillion won. Sales have more than doubled from 86.06 trillion won a year earlier.

The BBC, which put the profit at about $80bn, linked the record to the boom in chips used for artificial intelligence. Samsung makes the memory chips that sit next to AI processors in data centres. The BBC also expects a lift from the folding phones Samsung launched in August.

Samsung's guidance does not say how much each business contributed. Investors will look for that split in the full report.

3 things to know
  1. Samsung estimates Q3 operating profit at about 107.4 trillion won
  2. That is almost nine times the 12.17 trillion won of Q3 2025
  3. The figures are preliminary estimates, not final results
