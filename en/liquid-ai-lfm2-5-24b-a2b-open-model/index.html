Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-24B-A2B, an open agent model for laptops

The 24-billion-parameter model uses only 2.45 billion per token, supports Arabic among 16 languages and decodes 184 tokens a second on an M5 Max.

2026-10-14 · EmaritesAI

Liquid AI released LFM2.5-24B-A2B on October 13, with open weights on Hugging Face under its LFM 1.0 licence. The model has 24 billion parameters in total, but its mixture-of-experts design activates only about 2.45 billion for each token, which keeps it fast on laptops and desktops.

Liquid says the model is built for agents. It always reasons before answering and was trained inside agent tools such as OpenClaw and OpenCode. In the company's tests it scored 48.09 on the LiveClaw agent benchmark against 38.19 for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, a larger model, and it led all three instruction-following tests. Liquid also reports weaker results where bigger models still win: 74.42 on GPQA Diamond against Qwen's 83.69, and 51 on SWE-Bench Verified against 66.2.

The context window grows to 131,072 tokens from 32K in the previous version. The vocabulary doubled to 128K tokens to handle non-Latin scripts better, and Arabic is one of 16 supported languages. Pre-training used about 33 trillion tokens.

On Apple's M5 Max the model decodes 184 tokens per second, according to Liquid, and 114 on AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ 395. Day-one support covers llama.cpp, MLX, vLLM, SGLang and ONNX, with GGUF and 4-bit MLX builds available. OpenRouter also lists it at $0.05 per million input tokens and $0.70 per million output. All benchmark numbers are Liquid's own.
