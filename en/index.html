Meta brings its Muse AI assistant to the Mac

Meta's AI assistant Muse is now on macOS, where it can act inside native apps — files, messages, calendar, notes and mail. Access is opt-in per app and Meta says the assistant asks for approval before sensitive actions. Muse launched on mobile and web earlier this month and quickly topped the US App Store.
