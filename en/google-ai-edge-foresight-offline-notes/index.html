Google's free Foresight app takes meeting notes offline on Macs

The local-first notetaker transcribes meetings, answers questions with Gemma 4 and searches your own files.

2026-10-09 · EmaritesAI

Google has released AI Edge Foresight, a note-taking app for Macs with Apple Silicon that works entirely offline. The Verge says the app is free, and TechCrunch describes it as a local-first competitor to Granola, a popular AI meeting-notes app.

Foresight transcribes meetings, including in-person conversations, and shows your notes in a split-screen view beside the transcript. You can then chat with an assistant powered by Gemma 4, Google's family of open models, to ask about what was said or pull out details.

The app can also build a personal knowledge base from files on your computer. Supported formats include PDFs, Google Docs, Office files, plain text and Markdown, along with bookmarks. TechCrunch says the app uses EmbeddingGemma 2, a 740 million parameter model. Embedding models turn text into lists of numbers so that passages with related meaning can be found quickly.

Because Foresight works offline, transcription and the assistant run on the Mac itself rather than on Google's servers. That makes it an option for people who would rather not upload recordings of meetings to a cloud service.

The app comes from Google AI Edge, the company's platform for running AI models on devices, and has a page on Google's developer site at developers.google.com/edge/foresight.

3 things to know
  1. Google's AI Edge Foresight is a free, fully offline notetaker for Macs
  2. It transcribes meetings and answers questions with Gemma 4
  3. It can search your own PDFs, Docs, Office files and notes
Sources: TechCrunch, The Verge, Google for Developers: AI Edge Foresight
