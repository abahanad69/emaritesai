Qwen releases Image-2.1-Turbo, a 7B image model that needs 8 steps

The faster checkpoint creates and edits images, including transparent ones, and runs in Diffusers and Mac apps.

2026-10-12 · EmaritesAI

Alibaba's Qwen team has added a faster image model to Hugging Face. Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo, listed on October 9, is an accelerated checkpoint of Qwen-Image-2.1 and uses the same 7 billion parameter architecture for generating and editing pictures.

The main change is speed. Diffusion image models build a picture by removing noise over a series of steps, and many need dozens. According to the model card, Turbo uses a fixed 8-step schedule that loads automatically, with guidance set to 1 by default. It also reuses cached text and reference-image context between steps to cut repeated work. Qwen does not give timing figures or benchmark scores on the page.

The card shows text-to-image and editing across portraits, posters with typography, interface layouts and multi-image compositions. It can also produce transparent RGBA images, useful for logos and stickers. Aspect ratio presets run from 1536 by 2752 pixels for 9:16 to 2752 by 1536 for 16:9.

The weights are under the Qwen Research License rather than a standard open-source license, so anyone planning commercial use should read its terms first. The model runs through Hugging Face's Diffusers library and is listed for Google Colab, Kaggle and the Mac apps Draw Things and DiffusionBee. No hosted inference provider offered it at the time of writing.
