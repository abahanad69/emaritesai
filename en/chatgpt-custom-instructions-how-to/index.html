How to set up ChatGPT custom instructions in 5 steps

Tell ChatGPT once who you are and how you want answers, instead of repeating it in every chat.

2026-10-09 · EmaritesAI

Custom instructions let you tell ChatGPT once how you want it to respond, instead of repeating yourself in every chat. OpenAI's help centre says the feature is available on all plans.

On the web, open Settings, choose Personalization and switch on Enable customization. A Custom Instructions field then appears. On the mobile app, open Settings and choose Customize ChatGPT.

There is a length limit. Free and Go users get 1,500 characters. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu users get 5,000.

The most useful instructions are short and specific. Start with who you are and what you mostly use ChatGPT for, such as 'I teach science to 12-year-olds in Dubai'. Then describe the answers you want: length, tone, format, units and language. 'Keep answers under 100 words, use metric units and ask before assuming' works better than 'be helpful'.

You can switch custom instructions off at any time, which is useful when a task needs a different style. If answers start to feel off, read your instructions again: an old rule you forgot about may be the cause.

Step by step
  1. Open settings: On the web, go to Settings, then Personalization.
  2. Turn it on: Switch on Enable customization to show the Custom Instructions field.
  3. On mobile: Open Settings and choose Customize ChatGPT.
  4. Say who you are: Add your role and what you mostly use ChatGPT for.
  5. Set the style: Give length, tone, format and units; Free and Go allow 1,500 characters.
3 things to know
  1. Custom instructions set ChatGPT's style once for every new chat
  2. Web: Settings, Personalization, then Enable customization
  3. Limit: 1,500 characters on Free and Go, 5,000 on paid plans
Sources: OpenAI Help Center: ChatGPT Custom Instructions
⚠️ Settings path (web and mobile); available on all plans; can be turned off
⚠️ Limits: 1,500 characters Free/Go, 5,000 Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise/Edu
