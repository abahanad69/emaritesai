Anthropic tells investors Q3 revenue jumped 45% to $16.8B

The Claude maker shared the figure at a pre-IPO investor day in San Francisco, according to Reuters, Bloomberg and the BBC.

2026-10-15 · EmaritesAI

Anthropic told prospective investors that its revenue reached $16.8 billion in the third quarter, up 45% from the previous three months, according to Reuters, Bloomberg and the BBC. The figures were presented on Wednesday at a pre-IPO investor day at the company's San Francisco headquarters. Anthropic declined to comment to the BBC.

The growth has been steep all year. Revenue was $4.7 billion in the first quarter and $11.6 billion in the second, the reports said. Reuters, citing an investor who attended, said Anthropic was profitable on an adjusted basis in the quarter, and the BBC reported it was the second profitable quarter in a row. That is a sharp change from last year, when Reuters reported an operating loss of more than $8 billion.

Anthropic is preparing a stock market listing. Reuters reported the company is aiming for a valuation above $2 trillion and that the debut will likely come after the US midterm elections in November. Anthropic has also committed at least $518 billion over a decade to cloud, computing and infrastructure, according to Reuters.

The numbers arrived on a rough day for AI stocks. Chipmakers including Nvidia, AMD and Intel fell on Wednesday, and on Thursday morning SoftBank dropped 9% and Samsung 8.4%, the BBC reported, after Broadcom chief executive Hock Tan warned that some investors had set extreme expectations for AI spending. Anthropic's revenue, like OpenAI's, is closely watched as a test of whether that spending can pay off.

3 things to know
  1. Q3 revenue rose 45% to $16.8B, up from $4.7B in Q1 and $11.6B in Q2
  2. Reuters says Anthropic was profitable on an adjusted basis in Q3
  3. Listing likely after November's US midterms, at over $2 trillion
Sources: Reuters (via Investing.com), Bloomberg, BBC
