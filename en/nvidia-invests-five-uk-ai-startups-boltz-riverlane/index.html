Nvidia plans investments in 5 UK startups, from drug design to quantum

Boltz, Foresight, Hiverge, Prima Mente and Riverlane join a UK portfolio that already includes Wayve and Synthesia.

2026-10-15 · EmaritesAI

Nvidia said on Wednesday that it plans to invest in five more British AI startups, adding to a UK portfolio that already includes Basecamp Research, Synthesia and the self-driving company Wayve. The company did not disclose the size of the new investments.

The five cover a wide range. Boltz, a London startup founded by MIT researchers, builds biomolecular models and agents that predict how molecules interact and help design potential medicines. Foresight helps owners of large infrastructure projects, including hyperscale data centres, predict completion dates and manage risk. Hiverge, from former Google DeepMind and University of Cambridge researchers, builds a system that writes and optimises algorithms for tasks such as AI training and logistics. Prima Mente builds biological foundation models aimed first at Alzheimer's, Parkinson's and ALS. Cambridge-based Riverlane makes real-time error correction hardware and software for quantum computers.

Nvidia also pointed to a five-year, $250 million compute commitment for UK research on the safety and security of AI agents, and said it is joining the Earthshot Prize as a founding partner of its global alliance.

The announcement listed UK customers using Nvidia technology. Revolut's PRAGMA banking model was pre-trained on 26 million anonymised user histories. Fuse Energy is moving customer AI for more than 450,000 households onto its own Blackwell Ultra servers, and Liverpool City Region is building what it calls a sovereign "City Brain" for local services. Nvidia also highlighted NHS triage tools and a new open dataset of one million synthetic UK personas from Locai Labs.
