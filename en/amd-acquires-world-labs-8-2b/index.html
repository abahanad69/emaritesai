AMD to buy Fei-Fei Li's World Labs in $8.2B all-stock deal

Li becomes AMD's chief scientist as the chipmaker adds world-model research to take on Nvidia.

2026-09-29 · EmaritesAI

AMD has agreed to acquire World Labs, the AI research company co-founded by computer-vision pioneer Fei-Fei Li, in an all-stock deal valued at approximately $8.2 billion, the companies announced.

Li will join AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting to CEO Lisa Su, while the World Labs team continues its model research. The deal is expected to close by the end of the year.

World Labs launched in 2024 to build "world models", AI that understands and generates physical 3D environments rather than just text. Its first product, Marble, released in 2025, turns a prompt into an interactive 3D world and is also pitched for creating simulated environments to train robots.

AMD says understanding frontier workloads like these will shape its chip roadmap and its hardware, software and systems. The two companies already had an inference and training partnership from last year.

TechCrunch notes the acquisition helps AMD compete with Nvidia, which already offers open-weight world models such as Cosmos. World models are seen as key to deploying AI in robots, from autonomous vehicles to humanoids, where real-world training data is scarce.

3 things to know
  1. All-stock deal worth about $8.2 billion
  2. Fei-Fei Li becomes AMD EVP and chief scientist
  3. World models aim to give AMD an edge in robotics AI
Sources: The Verge, TechCrunch
