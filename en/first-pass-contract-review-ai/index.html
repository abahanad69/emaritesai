Read a contract faster with AI in 5 steps, and know where to stop

AI can map clauses and flag gaps, but the best legal models still fail most full tasks on a strict benchmark.

2026-10-13 · EmaritesAI

Legal AI is moving fast. This week Z.ai's GLM-Legal reported passing 19.2% of tasks on Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark, where a task counts only if every expert criterion is met. Even the top model on that board passes about a quarter. That is a useful guide: AI can speed up a first read, but it still misses things.

Start with privacy. Check whether the tool keeps or trains on what you upload, and remove names, account numbers and prices you are not allowed to share. The American Bar Association's Formal Opinion 512 warns lawyers that client data entered into self-learning tools can resurface later.

Then ask for structure, not a verdict. A good first prompt is: list each clause with its number, who it binds, and any dates, payments, renewals or penalties. Next, give the model your own checklist, such as termination, liability cap, governing law and notice period, and ask which are missing or unusual.

Make it quote. Ask the model to cite the clause number for every point so you can read the original text yourself. Finally, take the list of questions to a lawyer before you sign. ABA Opinion 512 says relying on AI output without independent checks can fall short of a lawyer's duty of competence.

Step by step
  1. Protect the data: Remove names, account numbers and prices, and check if the tool trains on uploads.
  2. Map the clauses: Ask for every clause with its number, who it binds, and its dates and payments.
  3. Bring a checklist: List the terms you need, like termination and liability cap, and ask what is missing.
  4. Make it cite: Require a clause number for every point so you can read the original wording.
  5. Hand it to a lawyer: Use the AI's list as questions for a professional before you sign anything.
3 things to know
  1. Remove private details and check the tool's data policy first
  2. Ask for a clause map and a check against your own list
  3. Have the AI cite clause numbers, then get a lawyer to review
