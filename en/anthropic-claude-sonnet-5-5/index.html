Anthropic launches Claude Sonnet 5.5: faster, cheaper to run

Same $2/$10 price as Sonnet 5, 30%+ faster, and close to Opus 5.5 on coding tests, Anthropic says.

2026-09-29 · EmaritesAI

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5 on Monday, its mid-tier model and a direct successor to Sonnet 5, which launched about three months ago.

Pricing is unchanged at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Anthropic says the new model runs more than 30% faster and costs up to 30% less for most work, because it typically needs far fewer tokens to finish the same task.

On Anthropic's own benchmarks, Sonnet 5.5 scores 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, ahead of Opus 5.5 at 66.4%, and 55.5% on CursorBench 4.0, just behind Opus 5.5's 57.8%. TechCrunch notes the company pitches it for everyday coding and office work.

Because its cyber capabilities are comparable to larger models, Sonnet 5.5 is the first Sonnet deployed with safeguards similar to those on Opus 5.5; higher-risk requests fall back to Sonnet 5. Developer Simon Willison reports it is now the model behind the free tier of claude.ai.

It is available in the Claude apps, through the API as claude-sonnet-5-5, and on AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure. Anthropic says Haiku 5.5 will follow in the coming weeks.
