Anthropic offers open-source projects free AI security scans

OSS Scanner is part of a new Cyber Mission that also brings Claude and engineers to critical infrastructure defenders.

2026-10-09 · EmaritesAI

Anthropic has launched what it calls the Anthropic Cyber Mission, a long-term programme to help defenders secure the systems people depend on. It starts with two areas: open-source software and critical infrastructure.

For open source, the company introduced OSS Scanner, an opt-in service inspired by Google's OSS-Fuzz. Projects that enrol get periodic security scans by Anthropic's most capable models at no cost. Each report includes a proof of concept showing how a bug could be exploited, an explanation and a suggested fix where one exists. Anthropic says the reports are sent without human review and may contain errors such as wrong severity ratings. It expects more than 90% of findings to be real, which is a forecast rather than a measured result.

The service is aimed at maintainers who can handle findings at scale. Smaller projects will keep receiving human-checked disclosures under Anthropic's existing policy.

For critical infrastructure, a Critical Infrastructure Defense Program brings Claude models, on-site engineers and threat research to companies that protect industrial systems. Its 11 founding partners include Accenture, CrowdStrike, Deloitte, Palo Alto Networks and Rockwell Automation.

Anthropic says it has already offered models and support to more than half of US states through a separate government programme launched in June.

3 things to know
  1. OSS Scanner gives open-source projects free periodic AI security scans
  2. Reports include a proof of concept and a suggested fix, unreviewed
  3. A critical infrastructure programme starts with 11 partners
