OpenAI releases GPT-6.1 Sol: near-Astra results at a fifth the price

The mid-tier model costs $2 in / $10 out per million tokens and is live in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API.

2026-09-30 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Sol at DevDay on Tuesday, just a week after GPT-6 Sol. The company says it delivers close to GPT-6 Astra's intelligence for agentic coding, computer use and professional work at one-fifth of Astra's standard API input and output prices.

API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.10. OpenAI says an "ultrafast" variant with up to 8x faster token generation is coming in the next few days.

On OpenAI's own evaluations, GPT-6.1 Sol scores 7 points higher than GPT-6 Sol on the OSWorld 2.0 computer-use benchmark and matches Astra on DeepSWE. At low reasoning effort, the share of answers with a factual error falls from 11.4% to 7.7%. OpenAI also says it is more reliable at following restrictions and flagging broken tools.

The model is available to Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and in the API as gpt-6.1-sol, but not yet in regular Chat.

It arrives in place of GPT-6.1 Astra, which OpenAI withheld this week after it failed internal safety tests.
