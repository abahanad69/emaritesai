How does Arena rank AI models from blind votes?

Two anonymous answers, one vote, and a statistics model that turns millions of match-ups into a score.

2026-10-09 · EmaritesAI

Arena, the leaderboard that raised $200 million this week, ranks models by asking people which answer is better.

A visitor writes a prompt and gets two answers from two models whose names stay hidden until after the vote. The visitor picks the better answer or calls a tie. Each vote is one match-up between two models, much like a game between two chess players.

Early on, the team used Elo, the chess rating system, which updates scores after every game. In December 2023 it switched to the Bradley-Terry model, which estimates every model's strength from all votes at once. The researchers said this gives more stable ratings and tighter confidence intervals.

Those intervals matter. A new model with few votes has a wide range, so two models a few points apart can be statistically tied. The ranking also reflects what voters prefer, which can include style and length, not only correctness.

Use Arena as one signal of how people experience a model, next to task benchmarks and your own tests.

Explained simply
  1. Blind match-up: You see two answers from hidden models and vote for the better one or a tie.
  2. Pairwise data: Each vote records one model beating, losing to or tying another.
  3. Rating model: Bradley-Terry fits all votes at once, replacing Arena's original Elo updates.
  4. Error bars: Fewer votes mean wider confidence ranges, so check overlaps before calling a winner.
  5. Read it right: Scores show human preference, which can reward style as well as accuracy.
3 things to know
  1. People vote between two anonymous answers to the same prompt
  2. A Bradley-Terry model turns all votes into scores with error bars
  3. Models a few points apart may be statistically tied
Sources: Chatbot Arena paper (arXiv 2403.04132), LMSYS: Chatbot Arena leaderboard update (Dec 2023), TechCrunch: Arena Series B
✅ Arena collects pairwise human votes between two model answers
⚠️ Switched from online Elo to Bradley-Terry for more stable ratings and precise confidence intervals
⚠️ Model names hidden until after the vote
