Ai2 open-sources AstaBrief 8B for fast, cited research reports

The small model turns a research question and paper excerpts into a cited report in one pass.

2026-10-03 · EmaritesAI

The Allen Institute for AI (Ai2) has released AstaBrief 8B, a compact open-weight model that writes cited reports from a research question and a set of retrieved paper excerpts. It runs today as the Fast mode of the Generate a report feature in Asta, Ai2's agentic platform for scientists, alongside a Claude-powered Thinking mode.

The aim is speed and control. According to Ai2, Fast mode averages 51.1 seconds per report across the full Asta pipeline, compared with 178.5 seconds for Thinking mode, roughly 3.5 times faster. Because the weights are open, universities and labs can run the model on their own servers, which Ai2 says matters when questions involve sensitive or unpublished work.

AstaBrief starts from Qwen3-8B. Ai2 says it collected about 90,000 research queries, filtered them to about 47,000 examples for supervised fine-tuning, and added roughly 6,000 preference pairs. It also redesigned the pipeline so the report is written in one pass instead of section by section. Alongside the weights, Ai2 is publishing the training data and an example workflow for building reports from local PDFs.

Ai2 notes that most training and evaluation were done in 2025, so its comparisons use proprietary models from that period rather than today's frontier systems. Quality results are the institute's own and have not been independently reproduced.
