Meta's Muse agent app passes 3.4M downloads in under three weeks

Sensor Tower data shows faster early growth than ChatGPT; Meta is opening early access to new features.

2026-09-28 · EmaritesAI

Meta's personal AI agent app, Muse, has passed 3.4 million downloads since launching on September 8, according to estimates from Sensor Tower reported by TechCrunch. Other trackers differ: Apptopia puts the total at 4.3 million and Appfigures at about 2.3 million. The app is currently available only in the US and Canada.

Sensor Tower says Muse averaged 55% day-over-day download growth in its first ten days, compared with 24% for ChatGPT at launch. It reached number one on the US App Store on September 18 and on Google Play a day later, and its daily active users rose 27% after Meta Connect.

Meta has promoted Muse heavily across Facebook and Instagram and on other networks, but Sensor Tower says ads accounted for only 6% of the app's impressions through September 19, suggesting much of the growth is organic.

At Connect, Meta previewed video chat with a Muse avatar, computer use on the Mac, a dedicated email address, more shopping partners and connectors, and voice control through its smart glasses. Users can now request early access by asking Muse directly.

The app's design is also drawing attention: The Verge found Muse will hand users a full copy of its cloud machine's filesystem. Meta says this is intended, since each user's Muse runs on its own Linux virtual machine in the cloud.

3 things to know
  1. 3.4M+ downloads since its Sept 8 launch (Sensor Tower)
  2. No. 1 on the US App Store since Sept 18
  3. Avatars, Mac computer use and glasses support are coming
Sources: TechCrunch, TechCrunch, The Verge
