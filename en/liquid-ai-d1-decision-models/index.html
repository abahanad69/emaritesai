Liquid AI releases d1, open models that decide without writing text

The 3B and 600M models answer yes-or-no, multiple-choice and rating questions in one pass, aimed at fast decisions on edge devices.

2026-10-09 · EmaritesAI

Liquid AI has released d1, a family of what it calls decision models. They are open-weight and available on Hugging Face from October 7, with day-one support in llama.cpp.

Unlike chatbots, d1 models do not write text. A developer gives them a state, such as a customer message, a JSON record, an image or a short audio clip, plus named questions with fixed answer types: yes or no, one option from a list, or a rating. The model returns all the answers in a single forward pass, with no generated tokens. A support team could, for example, ask in one call whether a message requests a refund, which team should handle it and how urgent it is.

There are two models. d1-3B is built on Liquid's LFM2.5-VL-3B and accepts text and images. d1-omni-600M, which Liquid calls an early experimental release, is built on LFM2.5-Encoder-350M with added vision and audio encoders, and takes text with an image or up to 30 seconds of speech. Its model card lists a 16,384-token context.

Liquid reports that d1-3B scores 48.57 on its Decision Index 0.2.1, on par with a mixture-of-experts model about 12 times larger. It puts single-question latency for d1-3B at 8 ms on an Nvidia RTX 4090 and 50 ms on a Jetson Orin Nano. These figures come from Liquid and have not been independently tested.
