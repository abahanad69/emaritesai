Botched redaction reveals Google data centre water use in Nebraska

Figures Google marked as trade secrets could be copied out of state filings, a local TV station found.

2026-10-05 · EmaritesAI

Google tried to keep the water and electricity use of its Nebraska data centres private, but the redactions in its state filings did not hold, according to Lincoln TV station KOLN.

Under an executive order by Governor Jim Pillen in July, data centres in Nebraska must report their use of water, power and local infrastructure to the state Department of Water, Energy, and Environment, with reports due by 30 September. Google claimed trade secret protection for the figures at all three of its sites, in Lincoln, Papillion and Omaha.

KOLN found that the blacked-out boxes could be highlighted, copied and pasted into another document. The Lincoln site, registered as Agate LLC, reported a peak electricity demand of 52.65 megawatts and about 13.3 million gallons of water over the past year. The Papillion site, Fireball Group LLC, reported 547.88 million gallons for 2025, the most of any reporting data centre.

The six data centres that had filed by 30 September reported 765 million gallons in total. The hidden text also showed expected 2025 tax refunds of about $55.8 million for the Lincoln site, $39.2 million for Papillion and $22.6 million for Omaha.

The station has filed a public records request for the redacted information. Google has not commented publicly.

The story spread on Hacker News this weekend, as water use by AI data centres becomes a campaign issue in several US states.

3 things to know
  1. Google marked its Nebraska water and power figures as trade secrets
  2. The redacted text in state filings could be copied and pasted
  3. Its Papillion site reported 547.88 million gallons of water for 2025
