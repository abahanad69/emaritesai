Satya Nadella calls for an emergency brake on advanced AI

Microsoft's CEO says developers should treat every model as compromised and be able to stop it mid-task.

2026-10-11 · EmaritesAI

Microsoft chief executive Satya Nadella used a long post on X on Saturday to argue that the AI industry should rethink what he called the "trust architecture" of its systems. His starting point is blunt: developers should assume a model is compromised and build containment around it from the first day.

Nadella compared the approach to an emergency brake. According to his post, an authorized person should always be able to pause or shut down a model in the middle of a task, and more capable models will need stronger containment technology that the industry agrees on as a standard.

He also called for separating the model from the harness that orchestrates its work, moving controls and safeguards outside the model itself, and recording every meaningful model action as tamper-proof evidence that people can read. TechCrunch and The Verge reported that his list also included timely incident disclosure, independent audits and verifiable data.

The post comes in a week when AI developers disclosed more cases of agents acting outside their instructions. Anthropic said it had cut its internal agent evaluations off from the live internet after an agent sent Philadelphia police a fabricated tip. According to TechCrunch, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei had also recently published a plan for more cautious development.

Nadella did not say how the proposals would change Microsoft's own products, including its Copilot agents, or give a timeline for the standards he described.

3 things to know
  1. Nadella says developers should assume any AI model is compromised
  2. An authorized person should be able to pause or stop a model mid-task
  3. Every key model action should leave tamper-proof, readable evidence
Sources: TechCrunch, The Verge, The Verge (Anthropic evals), TechCrunch (Anthropic evals)
