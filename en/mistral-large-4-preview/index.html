Mistral previews Large 4, a 1T-parameter open-weight model

The French lab's largest model is available through its API now, with weights promised by the end of October.

2026-10-07 · EmaritesAI

Mistral AI released a public preview of Mistral Large 4 on Tuesday, the largest model the Paris-based lab has built. The company calls it ML4, nicknamed Le Chonk, and describes it as a natively multimodal model with 1 trillion parameters, of which 49 billion are active for each token.

For now the model is only reachable through the preview API on Mistral Studio, priced at $1.36 per million input tokens and $4.18 per million output tokens. Mistral says the weights will follow by the end of October, after red-teaming with cybersecurity firms, vetted partners and state authorities.

Mistral trained ML4 from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in its own European data centres, according to its announcement. Mistral's VP of science Pierre Stock gave TechCrunch a rounder figure of about 4,000 GPUs. The company says more than 160 languages were in the training data.

The lab is pitching the model at security teams. In its own tests, ML4 scored 82% on a task that asks a model to reproduce and then patch a real software vulnerability, and solved 93% of Cybench challenges. Mistral also reports 61.7% on DeepSWE v1.1 for coding. These are vendor figures that have not yet been checked independently.

Mistral says it will publish more on the architecture, benchmarks and post-training before the weights ship. The launch follows a funding round in September that valued the company at about $24 billion, according to WIRED.
