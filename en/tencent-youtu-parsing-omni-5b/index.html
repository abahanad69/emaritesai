Tencent's 5B Youtu-Parsing-Omni turns docs, audio and video into JSON

One open model reads pages, charts, geometry figures, speech and video into a single structured output.

2026-10-10 · EmaritesAI

Tencent's Youtu team has released Youtu-Parsing-Omni, an open model that takes almost any file a business might need to read and returns it as structured JSON. The weights appeared on Hugging Face on October 9, along with a technical report and a plugin for the vLLM serving engine.

The model has 5 billion parameters and handles eight kinds of input: document pages, natural images, charts, flowcharts, geometry figures, audio, natural video and video with on-screen text. Depending on the prompt, it can return layout, text, tables, formulas, bounding boxes, timestamps and speech transcripts, or write captions and short reports. Tencent says one encoder serves all of these inputs, and that audio and video share the same timeline, so speech and frames line up.

On OmniDocBench v1.6, a document-parsing benchmark, Tencent reports an overall score of 96.96, just ahead of TeleOCR at 96.91 in its table. On its own broader OmniParsingBench, the model averages 75.08, behind Gemini-3-Pro at 77.44. These are vendor-reported results, and Tencent says its evaluation code is still to come.

The model runs on CUDA GPUs through vLLM, SGLang or Hugging Face Transformers. It ships under Tencent's own youtu-parsing license rather than a standard open-source license, so teams should read the terms before commercial use.
