OpenAI and Synopsys team up to build GPT-Synopsys for chip design

A multi-year deal aims to make a frontier model an expert user of chip design software.

2026-10-02 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI and Synopsys, one of the main suppliers of chip design software, have agreed to jointly build GPT-Synopsys, a model specialised in designing semiconductors. The companies announced the multi-year partnership on September 30.

Under the deal, OpenAI will license Synopsys' electronic design automation (EDA) tools to develop the model, and the two firms will share revenue and sell the product together. GPT-Synopsys will run on OpenAI-hosted infrastructure and is meant to work with customers' own agent systems.

The aim goes beyond today's setups, where general-purpose AI models are wired up to design tools. According to the announcement, GPT-Synopsys should learn to operate the tools the way an expert engineer would: running them, reading the results and refining a design repeatedly. Engineers would hand over goals such as improving power, performance and area, or closing timing and verification, and review what the agents produce.

This matters because chip design is slow, costly and short of specialists, while demand for AI chips keeps climbing. Synopsys CEO Sassine Ghazi framed the deal as speeding up design without sacrificing first-time-right silicon. OpenAI president Greg Brockman said better chips would in turn mean better AI.

The companies have not given a release date or pricing for GPT-Synopsys. Rival design-software makers are also adding AI agents, so the pace of delivery will be watched closely.
