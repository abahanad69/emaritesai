Google releases EmbeddingGemma 2 for on-device search in any media

The 740M-parameter open model maps text, code, images, video and audio into one shared space.

2026-10-07 · EmaritesAI

Google has released EmbeddingGemma 2, the second version of its small embedding model, and widened it from text to code, images, video and audio. Embedding models turn content into lists of numbers so software can find items with similar meaning, which is the basis of semantic search and retrieval for chatbots.

The model has 740 million parameters and is built on the Gemma 4 architecture, according to Google. It is modular: text-only use needs 270 million parameters, with optional vision (170 million) and audio (300 million) encoders. The weights are on Hugging Face and Kaggle under the Apache 2.0 license, which allows commercial use.

Google says the context window is 8,000 tokens, four times the first version, enough for about 5.5 minutes of audio or 29 images in one input. Output vectors can be cut from 768 dimensions down to 128 to save up to six times the storage. With quantization, Google measured about 191MB of RAM for the text-only model and 567MB for the full model on a Pixel 11 Pro.

On code search, Google reports a rise on the MTEB Code benchmark from 68.76 to 78.68. The first EmbeddingGemma passed 20 million downloads, Google says.

The model already works with tools including transformers, Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp and vLLM.
