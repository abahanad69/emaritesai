OpenAI posts a batch of AI-solved math problems on GitHub

The results come from an unreleased model, with Lean proofs attached, as mathematicians question how labs publish.

2026-10-07 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI published a new set of results on open problems in mathematics on Tuesday, produced by an internal frontier model it has not released. The company put the papers in a GitHub repository, along with proofs written in Lean, a language that lets a computer check each step of a proof.

According to The Verge, the release holds 722 manuscripts grouped into 372 result families. OpenAI says the average result used compute equal to about three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking, and it shared 10 summaries of the model's reasoning plus figures on how many problems it attempted.

In September OpenAI said the same model had resolved more than 100 long-standing open problems. The new batch gives the first detailed look at which ones. Mathematicians will need time to check the work, and Lean proofs only confirm the parts that have been formalized.

The release has reopened a dispute over how AI labs share research. An advisory group of mathematicians at the Institute for Advanced Study asked labs in late September to publish through academic channels where possible and to disclose models, prompts and compute. OpenAI says it drew on that advice. Northwestern mathematician Bryna Kra told WIRED that earlier input from mathematicians was ignored, and many researchers want journal-style papers instead of company posts.

OpenAI says it will keep updating the repository with more formal proofs and is looking at community-run places to host future results.
