Turn any meeting into a clear action list with AI

Five steps to get tasks, owners and deadlines out of a transcript, and to catch the mistakes AI makes.

2026-10-05 · EmaritesAI

Most video call tools can now transcribe a meeting, and AI assistants can turn that transcript into a list of decisions and next steps. Google Meet's "Take notes for me", for example, writes a document with Decisions and Next steps sections and saves it to the organiser's Drive. Its supported languages do not yet include Arabic, so bilingual teams in the Gulf often paste transcripts into a general chatbot instead.

The weak point is attribution. If the transcript assigns a sentence to the wrong speaker, the AI will give the task to the wrong person. Google's new DiarizationLM model, released this week, exists to fix exactly that kind of error.

A good prompt asks for structure. Request a table with four columns: task, owner, deadline and the quote it came from. Tell the AI to write "unassigned" or "no date" rather than guess. Ask for decisions separately from tasks, so a passing idea does not become a commitment.

Then check the list against the transcript, especially names and dates, and send it while the meeting is still fresh.

Before uploading any recording, tell participants and follow your organisation's rules on what can go into AI tools.

Step by step
  1. Get consent first: Tell everyone the meeting is being recorded or transcribed before you start.
  2. Fix speaker names: Check who said what in the transcript, since every task depends on it.
  3. Ask for a table: Request task, owner, deadline and source quote, with 'unassigned' where unclear.
  4. Split decisions from tasks: Keep agreed decisions apart so passing ideas don't become commitments.
  5. Verify, then send: Check names and dates against the transcript and share the list the same day.
3 things to know
  1. Ask for a table: task, owner, deadline and source quote
  2. Wrong speaker labels put tasks on the wrong person
  3. Check names and dates against the transcript before sending
