TikTok adds an AI shopping assistant and one-click checkout

Users can ask an AI agent about sizes and shipping, then buy from brands straight from the For You feed.

2026-10-06 · EmaritesAI

TikTok announced on Monday two new shopping features: an AI Shopping Assistant and a one-click checkout for buying directly from brands.

The Shopping Assistant is a conversational agent. TikTok says it keeps track of a user's preferences during a chat and can answer questions about product details, shipping, sizing and availability, then help complete the purchase. The checkout lets people buy from a brand in one click from the For You feed, the app's main video stream.

TikTok says it built the features with commerce and payment companies including Salesforce, Shopify, Shoplazza and Stripe. The move takes shopping beyond TikTok Shop, the in-app marketplace that has led its e-commerce push, and puts buying tools into the main feed.

An in-app assistant also gives users a reason to ask product questions inside TikTok rather than in outside chatbots such as ChatGPT.

The scale is large. TikTok's own Economic Impact Report, released last week, says activity on the app helped generate $81 billion in GDP for US businesses, and that 54 million Americans bought something after watching a TikTok video. Market research firm eMarketer estimates TikTok Shop made about $15.8 billion in US sales in 2025, roughly 18% of US social commerce.

TikTok has not said in which countries the features will launch first.

3 things to know
  1. TikTok's AI assistant answers questions on sizing, shipping and stock
  2. One-click checkout lets users buy from brands in the For You feed
  3. Partners include Salesforce, Shopify, Shoplazza and Stripe
