Reflection unveils Beam, a 501B open-weight model due this month

The US startup says its mixture-of-experts model matches GLM-5.2 on reasoning while using 3 to 4 times less inference compute.

2026-10-06 · EmaritesAI

Reflection AI, a Brooklyn-based startup founded in 2024 by two former Google DeepMind researchers, has announced Beam, its first open-weight model. Beam is a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 501 billion total parameters, of which 23 billion are active for each token. It handles text only and is aimed at coding, reasoning and agent tasks.

According to Reflection, Beam was pretrained on 23.8 trillion tokens. The company then ran a large reinforcement learning phase on 10,500 NVIDIA GB300 GPUs for four weeks, generating more than 100 million practice attempts across coding, agent and science tasks.

Reflection says Beam scores close to Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning tests while using 3 to 4 times less compute when answering, and that it trails larger open models such as Kimi K3 on raw capability. These are the company's own results and have not been independently checked. Users can set a reasoning effort level to trade answer length against cost.

The model is not yet downloadable. Reflection says Beam is in final red-teaming, early access is open by sign-up, and the weights, technical report and model card will follow later this month.

The startup has raised about $4.7 billion, according to PitchBook data cited by TechCrunch, and is pitching Beam to companies and governments that want to train their own local AI systems.
