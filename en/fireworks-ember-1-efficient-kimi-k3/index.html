Fireworks launches Ember-1, a leaner version of Kimi K3

Trained to cut unnecessary reasoning, it claims K3-level quality with about 40% fewer tokens.

2026-09-28 · EmaritesAI

Fireworks AI has released Ember-1, a specialised model that the company says delivers the quality of Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 while using about 40% fewer tokens.

The problem it targets is cost. Reasoning models like K3 can spend most of their output, sometimes more than 90%, on internal reasoning rather than the answer. In multi-turn agent workloads that reasoning is replayed on every turn, so context and cost balloon. Fireworks says simply lowering K3's reasoning-effort setting sacrificed too much quality.

Instead, the company retrained the model to drop unnecessary reasoning while keeping useful self-correction. It says it ran more than 50 training experiments and 200 evaluations on its own Serverless Training platform, using its own data and no customer data. Training covered maths, coding, instruction following, search, tool use and software engineering.

Across seven public benchmarks and live A/B tests on two customers' production traffic, Fireworks reports that K3's reasoning was shortened by 35-50% without losing accuracy. Ember-1 is available now and is the first in a planned series of models from Fireworks Research.

3 things to know
  1. Built on Kimi K3, retrained to trim wasted reasoning
  2. Claims K3-level quality with about 40% fewer tokens
  3. Reasoning cut 35-50% on benchmarks and live traffic
Sources: Fireworks AI
