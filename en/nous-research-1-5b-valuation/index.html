Nous Research hits $1.5B valuation and launches agents for business

The maker of the open-source Hermes agent raised $90 million in a Series B led by Robot Ventures.

2026-10-08 · EmaritesAI

Nous Research, the three-year-old company behind the open-source Hermes agent, has confirmed a $90 million Series B at a $1.5 billion valuation, TechCrunch reports. Robot Ventures led the round, with Nvidia, Union Square Ventures, Menlo Ventures, Samsung and 1789 Capital also taking part. The company has now raised $158 million in total.

Alongside the funding, Nous launched Hermes for Businesses, which lets companies deploy customised AI agents for multi-step workflows while keeping their data private. TechCrunch did not report pricing or a rollout date.

The startup says its open-source Hermes agent has been cloned more than 24 million times, and estimates that Hermes drives roughly 2.5% of global AI token usage. Both figures are the company's own and have not been independently checked. TechCrunch also notes that one of the investors, 1789 Capital, counts Donald Trump Jr. as a partner.

According to The Wall Street Journal, as cited by TechCrunch, Nous had about $36 million in annualised revenue in mid-September and expects to pass $100 million before the end of 2026. Reaching that target would mean almost tripling its revenue run rate in roughly three and a half months.
