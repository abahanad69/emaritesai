OpenAI in talks to raise $30B at $1.4T; Altman ties IPO to safety

Bloomberg reports a pre-IPO bridge round; Altman says OpenAI won't list until it can make confident safety claims.

2026-09-30 · EmaritesAI

OpenAI is in talks with investors to raise at least $30 billion in a pre-IPO round at a valuation of roughly $1.4 trillion, Bloomberg reported on Tuesday, according to TechCrunch. The raise would serve as a bridge to a public listing.

Bloomberg reports that OpenAI's run-rate revenue reached $40 billion in August, up 70% since July, helped by a renewed focus on areas such as coding. The company raised $122 billion in March at an $852 billion valuation, a round that was expected to be its last before going public.

Speaking to reporters after his DevDay keynote, chief executive Sam Altman said OpenAI will not go public until it can make confident safety claims about its models, The Verge reports. He said he did not want additional pressure from Wall Street during a shift to far more capable models, while also warning that waiting too long would be bad for the world. He has already ruled out an IPO in 2026.

The comments come a day after OpenAI withheld GPT-6.1 Astra over safety concerns, and as rival Anthropic's own IPO filing puts a spotlight on the risks. OpenAI did not respond to TechCrunch's request for comment on the round.

3 things to know
  1. Talks to raise $30B+ at ~$1.4T valuation (Bloomberg)
  2. Run-rate revenue reportedly $40B in August
  3. Altman: no IPO until OpenAI can make confident safety claims
