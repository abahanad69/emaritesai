AI leaderboard Arena nearly doubles its valuation to $3.1B

The Berkeley-born ranking site raised $200 million and launched a leaderboard that tests how AI agents behave.

2026-10-09 · EmaritesAI

Arena, the AI leaderboard that began as a research project at UC Berkeley in 2023, has raised a $200 million Series B at a $3.1 billion valuation, TechCrunch reports. In January, its $150 million Series A valued the company at $1.7 billion after the money came in.

Lightspeed and Khosla led the new round. Salesforce Ventures, 01 Advisors, Dell Technologies Capital, Endeavor Catalyst, a16z and Felicis also took part.

Arena ranks AI models by showing people two anonymous answers side by side and asking them to vote for the better one. TechCrunch says the site draws tens of millions of visitors a month. It reports that Arena reached $100 million in annualised run-rate revenue in June, up from $30 million at the time of the January round. Annualised revenue is a projection from recent sales, not money earned over a full year.

Alongside the funding, Arena published a new alignment leaderboard. It measures how models behave when acting as agents, scoring three failures: unauthorised actions, false attribution and deceptive completion, where a model reports a task as done when it is not. OpenAI models sit at the top of the first version. Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 ranks sixth and Claude Fable ninth.

The funding figures come from TechCrunch's report and have not been independently confirmed.
