Anthropic gives startups a free year of Claude Team, $1,000 credits

The expanded Claude for Startups program is open to companies founded in the last five years or funded in the last two.

2026-10-07 · EmaritesAI

Anthropic has expanded Claude for Startups, its program for young companies building with its models. The new version was announced on Tuesday at SF Tech Week, TechCrunch reports.

Approved startups get one year of Claude Team, Anthropic's paid plan for groups, free for up to five premium seats if they are new to Team. They also get $1,000 in Claude API credits with higher rate limits, according to the program page.

The package adds bi-weekly office hours with Anthropic's Applied AI team, access to Claude Marketplace for building plug-ins, and up to $45,000 in offers from partner companies through what Anthropic calls the Claude Startup Stack. Founders backed by partner venture firms can get up to $100,000 in extra API credits.

Companies qualify if they were founded in the last five years or received funding in the last two, and venture backing is not required, so bootstrapped firms can apply. Applicants need a Claude Console account and a company email address. Anthropic says most decisions arrive within minutes, while manual reviews take two to three business days.

Anthropic said in its announcement that it expects most people to feel the benefits of AI through companies building on top of models.

3 things to know
  1. Free year of Claude Team for up to 5 premium seats
  2. $1,000 in Claude API credits, up to $100K more via partner VCs
  3. Open to startups founded in 5 years or funded in the last 2
