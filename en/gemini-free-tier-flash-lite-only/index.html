Google limits free Gemini users to Flash-Lite from October 9

Flash moves behind the $4.99 AI Plus plan, and Pro and Deep Think will need AI Pro or Ultra.

2026-10-07 · EmaritesAI

Google is cutting what free users get in its Gemini app. Starting October 9, anyone on the free plan will be limited to Gemini Flash-Lite, the smallest and fastest of the three models in the app, according to The Verge and Android Headlines.

Free users can currently pick Flash-Lite, Flash or Pro. After the change, the standard Flash model will need a Google AI Plus subscription at $4.99 a month. AI Plus is also losing access to Gemini Pro, and Google says those subscribers will get an email telling them when the change applies to them.

Gemini Pro and the Deep Think reasoning option will then sit only in the Google AI Pro plan, at $19.99 a month, and AI Ultra, at $99.99. Deep Think was previously limited to Ultra, so AI Pro subscribers gain it.

Google says users on every plan can still choose low, medium or high effort for each model, but higher effort uses up the usage limit faster. Google's own help page describes Flash-Lite as a model built for speed and everyday tasks such as summaries and brainstorming, while Pro is aimed at complex maths and coding.

Google has not given a public reason for the change. Running large models is expensive, and the move pushes more regular users toward paid plans.

3 things to know
  1. Free Gemini accounts get only Flash-Lite from October 9
  2. Flash needs Google AI Plus at $4.99 a month, which loses Pro
  3. Pro and Deep Think move to AI Pro ($19.99) and Ultra ($99.99)
