Anthropic commits $11.6B to Akamai cloud over seven years

The largest deal in Akamai's history is a bet on CPUs as agents take on more work.

2026-09-28 · EmaritesAI

Anthropic has agreed to spend $11.6 billion over seven years on Akamai's cloud infrastructure, Akamai said on Thursday, TechCrunch reports. That is more than six times the $1.8 billion deal between the two companies that Bloomberg reported in May, and the largest contract in Akamai's history.

The commitment is not unconditional. According to Akamai's securities filing, it depends on Akamai meeting delivery and service-availability requirements, and either side can end the agreement under certain conditions.

The deal is also a bet on a less-hyped part of AI infrastructure: CPUs, the general-purpose chips that run code, browse the web and handle much of the work agents do between calls to a model. Akamai did not say what Anthropic will use the capacity for.

Akamai will not book revenue from the deal this year. Executives told investors they expect $150 million to $300 million in 2027, starting in the second half, rising to an annual pace of about $1.7 billion by the end of 2028. To build the capacity, Akamai expects to spend about $5.5 billion, and it is adding roughly $1.7 billion to this year's capital spending to buy components such as memory early. As part of the agreement, Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant for nonvoting preferred stock.

3 things to know
  1. $11.6B over seven years, over 6x the deal reported in May
  2. Commitment depends on Akamai meeting delivery and availability terms
  3. Revenue starts in 2027, reaching ~$1.7B a year by end of 2028
