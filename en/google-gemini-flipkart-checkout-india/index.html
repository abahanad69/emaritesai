Google tests Flipkart checkout inside Gemini in India

A 'Buy' button in Gemini and AI Mode sends select shoppers straight to Flipkart's checkout.

2026-09-28 · EmaritesAI

Google has started testing direct purchases from Flipkart inside its AI products in India, moving Gemini from recommending products to closing the sale, TechCrunch reports.

Users in the test see a "Buy" button on select Flipkart listings in Gemini and in Google's AI Mode. Tapping it opens Flipkart's checkout flow without leaving the AI interface, according to people familiar with the matter and an experience seen by TechCrunch.

The test is limited to some users and a small range of products, including smartphones, electronics and mobile accessories. Other users still see standard Flipkart listings with no in-chat purchase option. One person told TechCrunch that Google plans a broader rollout later in October, ahead of India's festive shopping season, one of the busiest retail periods of the year.

Flipkart is owned by Walmart, which makes the partnership a notable one in a market where Amazon is a major rival.

Asked about the test, a Google spokesperson said only that the company is "always testing new features and experiences."

The move is part of a wider race: Google and rivals including OpenAI are adding commerce to their assistants, betting that the assistant that answers the shopping question should also take the order.

3 things to know
  1. Buy button on select Flipkart listings in Gemini and AI Mode
  2. Test limited to some users and electronics categories
  3. Wider rollout reportedly planned before India's festive season
Sources: TechCrunch
