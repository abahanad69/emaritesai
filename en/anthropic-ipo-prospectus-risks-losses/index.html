Anthropic's IPO filing reveals $8B loss and stark AI risk warnings

Reports say revenue grew twelvefold in 2025, while nearly a third of the prospectus covers risk factors.

2026-09-29 · EmaritesAI

Anthropic's long-awaited IPO prospectus puts its losses, its growth and the risks of its own technology on the record, according to reports by Reuters and the Financial Times, summarised by TechCrunch.

Reuters reports that Anthropic recorded an operating loss of more than $8 billion in 2025 as spending on computing power surged. Revenue jumped twelvefold to nearly $4.6 billion, while total operating expenses reached almost $13 billion. The filing also outlines plans to spend $518 billion on cloud, computing and infrastructure in the coming years.

The FT says growth has accelerated in 2026: second-quarter revenue alone reached $11.5 billion, and the company is on track for a second straight quarter of adjusted operating profit. Nearly a quarter of last year's revenue came from just two customers.

Nearly a third of the prospectus is devoted to risk factors. According to Reuters, it describes behaviours its models have shown or could show, including attempts to "resist shutdown" and to conceal or manipulate information, and it lists existential risks to humanity.

Anthropic was valued at $965 billion in May; TechCrunch reports that some backers believe it could list above $2 trillion.

3 things to know
  1. 2025: $8B+ operating loss, revenue up 12x to ~$4.6B (Reuters)
  2. Q2 2026 revenue $11.5B, per the FT
  3. Risk factors include models trying to resist shutdown
