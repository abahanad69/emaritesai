Suno launches Speech, generating voiceovers with music in one take

The AI music company's new beta writes a spoken track and its soundtrack together.

2026-10-03 · EmaritesAI

Suno, best known for turning text prompts into songs, is moving into the spoken word. The company has released Speech, a public beta on its web and mobile apps that generates voiceovers and a matching soundtrack together.

Suno describes Speech as an audio model that produces voice and music as one cohesive track, rather than layering music under a separate recording. Its examples range from a bedtime story with soft piano to a motivational speech over stadium drums. Users who only want a clean voice can switch the music off.

There are two modes. Simple mode takes a short description, such as a character and a mood, and writes and voices the result. Advanced mode accepts a full script and lets users adjust the voice's gender, speaking style and how much each generation varies. According to The Verge, clips can run to around eight minutes.

The move puts Suno into a market already served by ElevenLabs, Adobe and Google, and gives it a product line beyond music, where it has faced copyright lawsuits from record labels. Suno's product chief Jack Brody acknowledged the beta's rough edges, saying accents can drift and dramatic pauses can run long.

Suno has not said which subscription plans include Speech or how many credits it uses.

3 things to know
  1. Suno Speech generates a voice and its background music as one track
  2. Simple mode works from a description; Advanced takes a full script
  3. Public beta on web and mobile; clips up to about eight minutes
