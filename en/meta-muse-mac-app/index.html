Meta brings its Muse AI assistant to the Mac

The desktop app works inside native files, mail, messages, calendar and notes, with opt-in access.

2026-09-19 · EmaritesAI

Meta's Muse assistant is now available on the Mac, extending an app that launched on mobile and the web earlier this month and quickly climbed to the top of the US App Store charts.

On macOS, Muse can interact with files, messages, calendar, notes and mail inside their native applications rather than in a separate chat window. Access is granted per app on an opt-in basis, and Meta says the assistant will always ask for approval before performing sensitive actions.

The release lands in the middle of a fast-moving race for consumer AI agents, which many in the industry expect to reshape how people use computers — and, eventually, what software and hardware they need at all. Rivals are shipping at a similar pace: Muse and the assistant Instinct, reported to be raising at a $10 billion valuation, both added voice calling in the same week.

Announcing the Mac app on X, Mark Zuckerberg said "The team is shipping fast."

For now the open question is distribution rather than capability. Consumer agents are converging on a similar feature set, and the winner is likely to be whoever reaches the most people first.

  2. Per-app opt-in access; approval requested before sensitive actions
  3. Launched on mobile and web this month and topped the US App Store
