Nvidia in talks to buy or invest more in $25B Reflection AI

The Financial Times reports the deal could take the form of an acquihire, days after Reflection unveiled its 501B open model Beam.

2026-10-12 · EmaritesAI

Nvidia is in talks to either buy Reflection AI outright or put more money into the startup, the Financial Times reported on October 10, citing people with direct knowledge of the discussions. Bloomberg and Reuters followed with their own write-ups of the FT report. Neither company has commented publicly, and the FT said the terms under discussion could not be established.

One option on the table is an acquihire, where a buyer hires a startup's team and licenses its technology instead of buying the whole company. According to the FT, that structure could help Nvidia avoid a full antitrust review. Nvidia already backs Reflection, and the startup trained on Nvidia chips.

Reflection was founded by former Google DeepMind researchers Misha Laskin and Ioannis Antonoglou. It was valued at $25 billion in a funding round in March, the FT reported. Earlier this month it introduced Beam, a 501 billion parameter open-weight model, with the weights due to be published in October.

The talks matter beyond one company. US officials have pointed to Reflection as a domestic answer to cheaper Chinese open models such as DeepSeek. A deal would also move Nvidia, the main supplier of AI chips, further into building the models that run on them, alongside its own Nemotron family. Nothing has been signed, and the talks could still end without a deal.
